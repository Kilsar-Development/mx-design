## Loading overlay (24 to 25 Nov 2026)
Lives in `Mx Web Login.dc.html`. Joel's brief: "a full screen overlay that blurs the underlying content, with some element in the middle that shows activity", "design something creative in line with our ds". Timing and flicker handling are already built in the product and are not to be designed. Direction he set: "playful and organic"; "as we push deeper into the generated content arena, including 3d models, animations, etc, creativity is going to be a central feel"; "our app is focusing on measurable knowledge gain by its users as well as auto creation of new 3d content"; no logo ("it would be cool to convey, innovation, or inspiration and creativity"). He allowed opening the login page in the viewer "as needed" to see the animations.

Tries, in order:
1. Kilsar mark traced by two strokes on a 120 disc. Rejected: "too busy", no logo.
2. One small `primary` shape on an 88 disc, two candidates (spark, bloom). He picked bloom and asked for "more movement? and color? like blooms changing in to other shapes, and then back".
3. Four petals (`primary`, `accent1`, `inversePrimary`, `accent4`) that open, stretch into a flower, become diamonds and fold back while the figure turns. "getting there", but faster, and "with the zoom and changing combo...it looks more like morphing and distortion vs creation".
4. Four round pieces that pop in and hop between a bloom, a block, a line and a triangle. "now it just seems to convey keeping the same info, but rearranging it. not really what we are going for."
5. Sketch becomes solid. "i like this direction"; after the colour pass, "nice. perfect!". This is the final design.

Final design: `scrim` plus a 12px blur over the whole screen; a 104 round `surface` disc with the drop shadow; inside, a thin `onSurface` line draws the outline of a simple 3D object, its faces fill in, it holds, clears, and the next is drawn (cube, pyramid, cylinder; 2.2s each, 6.6s for the three). Each face is a gradient between two roles of one family (`primaryContainer` to `inversePrimary`, `primary` to `onPrimaryContainer`, `accent1` to `accent4`); the cylinder wall first ran dark-light-dark and Joel found it "out of place against the others", so it uses the same subtle `primary` to `onPrimaryContainer` blend. Faces are 92% opaque and a faint `shadow` ellipse sits under each object. Faces settle with a bouncy curve, `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`; lines use the standard curve.

Not in the design system, told to Joel, not yet added to it: the 12px blur, the loop lengths, the bouncy curve, gradients and partial opacity.

Technically it is an inline SVG animated by two CSS keyframe animations, no script. Joel did not want a separate spec; the Flutter suggestions (one CustomPainter and one AnimationController, path metrics for the line drawing, gradient shaders for the faces, a RepaintBoundary, paths built once) are in an IMPLEMENTATION NOTE comment above the overlay in the page. Viewed in the viewer as frame bursts in light and dark.

## Design Studio (brought in 20 Nov 2026)
Joel asked for Design Studio, a new feature in the original MX mock, to be brought into the Design Studio tab for instructor and admin, on MX DS V3 and the Mx Web look. - It is NOT written in the page template. It is a React component file, `design-studio.jsx`, mounted by `Mx Web.dc.html` with `<x-import component-from-global-scope="DesignStudio" from="./design-studio.jsx" theme="{{ theme }}">` when the role is instructor or admin and the route is `designstudio`. - Both roles get the identical screen. Sessions reset to the samples when you leave the tab.
- What is in it: sessions list (Active / Archived, rename, archive, delete, New session dialog); session workspace (conversation with a simulated assistant, Library document picker, Task draft editor, 3D content viewer with playback, save to Library, discard, cancel, retry, Add to a Task dialog). - Adherence: styles are objects, so the check works line by line. Font size, weight and letter spacing may be written ONLY in the `TX` table between the two `ds-check:text-tokens` markers; everything else uses `tx("tokenName", colour)`. The check also verifies that table against the 16 tokens.

## Mx Mobile (started 16 Nov 2026)
`Mx Mobile.dc.html` is the phone mock (390 × 844, light and dark), a sibling of Mx Web on MX DS V3 and under the same check. Joel's brief: mobile is for everything web is for; all three roles, student first; every feature except Training (web only for now); phone only, tablet later; minimum touch zone 32, up to 40 or 44 as needed. He asked for an independent design, so the current mobile product designs were deliberately not looked at.

### Navigation framework (first draft, awaiting Joel's review)
- One frame for every role: device status bar · app bar (mark, workspace and group, search, bell, avatar) · scrolling content · bottom bar.
- Bottom bar, up to five items: Home · Work · Assessments · Orion · More. Items come and go by role and by feature: Orion drops out when the feature is off; Work shows a Tasks / Jobs switch only when Jobs is on; More lists everything else in groups (Learn, Knowledge, Account) and hides what is off.

- Student Home, in order: Continue (the one thing to do now) · Next exam (questions seen, practice average, weak area) · Your path · assigned work with To do / Overdue / Completed chips · Start here.
- Work, Assessments, Orion and More are first-pass landing screens so the navigation works. Everything they open shows a "Not designed for mobile yet" toast.

### Mobile sizes used in the draft (PROPOSED, not yet approved, not in the design system)
