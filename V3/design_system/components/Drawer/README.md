# Drawer

The place for editing and for detail that should not take the user off the page. It slides in from the right over a `scrim`, `sizingSmall` in from the top, right and bottom edges.

- **Standard**: 460px. Forms, settings, notifications, the worklog thread.
- **Wide**: 50vw, between 620px and 820px. Content-heavy editors (assignment, question, generate).
- Header with a `titleBold` title and the close button; a scrolling body with `sizingMed` between fields; a footer with Cancel and one primary button, right-aligned.

A drawer that lists a history widens with the comparison pane opening to its left. Dialogs are reserved for blocking confirmation.
